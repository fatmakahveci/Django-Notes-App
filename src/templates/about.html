A useful discovery, a half-formed idea, a few lines about your day. Django Notes gives you somewhere to put them and a way to find them again.

Write now, come back later

Create an account to start a note. Add a title and some text, then save it. Categories are optional. Use My notes to pick up where you left off, edit a note, or delete one you no longer need.

Choose who can read it

New notes default to Published, which means anyone can read them. Choose Draft before saving to keep a note visible only to you and site administrators. When you want to share it, edit the note and change its visibility to Published.

While you type, a private recovery copy keeps your work in progress. Watch the save status and choose Save to apply your changes; a recovery copy never publishes a note.

Find it again, or take it with you

Search by title or text, narrow results by date, or organize your own notes with personal tags and pins. Select several notes to update them together. Headings, lists, emphasis, and links stay with your writing.

Made a mistake? Restore a previous saved version from Version history, or recover a deleted note from Trash. Both return your note as a private draft.

Copy a note, download it as text or Markdown, or print it. Import / export in My notes lets you take a Markdown backup of your collection and bring notes back as new drafts.
